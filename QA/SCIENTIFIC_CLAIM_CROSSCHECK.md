# Scientific Claim Crosscheck

The three public reproduction scripts are the executable crosscheck. They must exit
zero and reconstruct the Paper 1 mean rank agreement/repeat state, Paper 2 same-cell
viability contradiction, and Paper 3 replay/state ledger without fitting models.

Canonical boundaries:

- JARVIS: local numerical replay; byte divergence remains disclosed.
- Materials Project: admission and viability remain held/conditional; 916 elastic replay
  exceedances remain disclosed.
- Matbench: exact same-environment repeat across 65,740 numeric cells.
- Spatial V0.5: exact local replay of its declared route.
- Spatial V0.6.1: exact byte, semantic and numerical replay; bulk geometry PASS in both
  views; shear polarity conditional with no cross-view pooling.
- R2 uses `sklearn.metrics.r2_score` and the mean of evaluated held-out `y_true`.
- Materials empirical validation does not establish cross-domain scientific validation.

Execution results are appended to `REPRODUCTION_EXECUTION_RESULTS.md` during QA.
