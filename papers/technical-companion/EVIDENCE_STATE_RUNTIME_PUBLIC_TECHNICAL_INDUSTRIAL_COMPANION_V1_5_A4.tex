\callout{Core proposition.}{Computational evidence should be treated as a stateful object. A trustworthy workflow records what entered, what executed, what completed, what resumed, what was separately identified as replay, what failed, what is retained as scientific evidence, and what downstream action that evidence has earned.}

\callout{Industrial question.}{Can an organisation prove what happened, recover interrupted work without changing scientific identity, reproduce the relevant decision, contain unsupported output, and show who authorised the next action?}

\callout{This companion in plain language.}{\textbf{Question:} how can a long computational workflow show what actually completed, what safely resumed, what repeated, and what action was authorised? \textbf{What was exercised:} atomic scientific units, interruption recovery, multi-contract replay, separated memory, and human-controlled evidence updates. \textbf{Why it matters:} a successful process exit is not the same as accepted scientific evidence or engineering permission. \textbf{Boundary:} this is local implementation and exercise, not enterprise qualification or a public software distribution.}

\callout{Why move beyond materials ML?}{Materials ML provides the deepest scientific evidence because its sources, models, controls, metrics, and replay states are inspectable. The first asks whether an interpretation is justified. The second asks what a result is allowed to change next.}

\section{Scientific method and runtime assurance are different}
A scientific method states what is measured: the source data, cohort, representation, model, split, controls, metrics, and decision rule. Runtime assurance records execution identity, completed units, safe resume, separately identified replay, and the passed comparison contract.

Neither substitutes for the other. A perfectly logged poor method remains poor science. A sound method with missing, duplicated, or ambiguous outputs remains incomplete evidence.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.21\textwidth}YY}
\toprule & Scientific method & Runtime assurance\\\midrule
Primary question & What is being tested, against what comparator, and under which assumptions? & Did the frozen test execute completely and reproduce under the declared contract?\\
Typical objects & Dataset, cohort, features, model, hyperparameters, folds, metrics, nulls, thresholds & Execution identity, unit plan, lock, journal, output contract, hashes, terminal seal, replay comparison\\
Failure example & Leakage, unsuitable target, weak comparator, post-hoc threshold & Partial output, duplicate unit, identity drift, corrupt checkpoint, replay mismatch\\
Authority & Supports the scientific interpretation only if its design is adequate & Supports only the declared completion/recovery/replay state\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\callout{Authority rule.}{A weaker evidence state does not inherit the authority of a stronger one merely because both occur in the same workflow.}

\section{Worked public example: exact replay preserves a refusal}
The final 256-material spatial confirmation tested two predeclared representation questions with Ridge models. For bulk modulus, continuous metric geometry had to exceed both a baseline and a matched sham by at least $0.05$ mean $R^2$ in each of two population views. For shear modulus, polarity information had to exceed geometry and its sham by at least $0.02$ in each view.

The fresh execution committed 576/576 scientific units. A separately identified replay committed another 576/576. All 1,728 corresponding output pairs were byte-identical. Semantic identity passed, and 927,361 numeric cells matched exactly.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.18\textwidth}YYYY}
\toprule Question & Continuity view & Chemical-system view & Frozen decision & Practical reading\\\midrule
Bulk geometry & $+0.4778$ vs baseline; $+0.5145$ vs sham & $+0.4855$ vs baseline; $+0.5157$ vs sham & PASS both views & Compact metric geometry earned retention for this bounded route.\\
Shear polarity over geometry & $+0.0123$; below $+0.02$ & $+0.0453$; above $+0.02$ & Conditional; cross-view FAIL & One view could not repair failure in the other.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Why this matters industrially.} Exact replay proved that the mixed decision itself repeated. It did not grant permission to turn the failed cross-view polarity claim into a universal success. This is the practical purpose of separating execution quality from scientific outcome.

\section{Evidence-state control model}
\begin{center}\small\bfseries
SOURCE MEMORY $\downarrow$ NATIVE DOMAIN EXECUTION $\downarrow$ EXECUTION MEMORY $\downarrow$ CANDIDATE EVIDENCE $\downarrow$ REPLAY / ADJUDICATION $\downarrow$ HELD OR ACCEPTED EVIDENCE $\downarrow$ SEPARATELY AUTHORISED ACTION
\end{center}
The domain supplies meanings, admission rules, models, and application thresholds. The portable layer preserves identity, completion, comparison, lineage, and action state around those declared objects.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.18\textwidth}YY}

\section{Atomic execution and recovery}
The reference design binds source, configuration, dependencies, ordered units, and expected outputs into an execution identity. A unit advances only after its complete output set passes verification. Incomplete staging is never reused as completed evidence.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.22\textwidth}YY}
\toprule Control & Public behaviour & Industrial consequence\\\midrule
Single writer & One process owns a declared execution & Concurrent processes cannot silently own the same run\\
Atomic completion & Complete outputs accepted only after validation & Partial files cannot impersonate completion\\
Hash-linked journal & Checkpoint events bind continuation to prior state & Continuation lineage remains inspectable\\
Terminal verification & Outputs and receipts rechecked before sealing & Post-run inconsistency remains detectable\\
Resume & Same unsealed identity; verified units skipped; incomplete units rerun & Interruption does not create a new scientific story\\
Replay & New identity and predecessor; no predecessor output reuse & Repeatability becomes separately inspectable\\
Duplicate/output refusal & Missing, duplicate, unexpected, or schema-invalid output fails & Restart cannot silently double-count or accept extras\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\callout{Operational distinction.}{Resume proves recoverability. Replay tests predecessor-linked repeatability. A resumed run must never be described as a separately identified repeat or external replication.}

\section{Multi-contract replay}
Reproducibility is not one Boolean flag. A route can be scientifically numerically equivalent while its files differ in serialization, or byte-identical while reproducing a failed scientific threshold.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.23\textwidth}YY}
\toprule Contract & Question & Failure meaning\\\midrule
Plan identity & Did the same complete ordered workload execute? & Subset, superset, or reordered route\\
Source/configuration & Were frozen inputs and settings preserved? & Different scientific identity\\
Schema/coverage & Is every expected object present exactly once? & Incomplete or ambiguous output\\
Semantic identity & Do values refer to corresponding scientific cells? & Scientifically misaligned comparison\\
Numerical equivalence & Do numeric cells satisfy the frozen rule? & Numerical replay contract failed\\
Raw-byte identity & Are payloads byte-identical? & Storage divergence; not automatically scientific divergence\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Bounded authority and detachable generative AI}
The negative route is deliberately simple: unsupported request $\rightarrow$ HOLD $\rightarrow$ zero accepted-memory writes $\rightarrow$ no downstream action. The positive route requires verified evidence, explicit human authorisation, bounded persistence, read-back verification, and preserved reversible history.

AI-assisted development is not AI-dependent scientific execution. Conventional fitted models are part of the science. Generative systems may orient, critique, diagnose, or propose, but receive no permission to promote their own output into accepted evidence or engineering action.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.23\textwidth}YY}
\toprule Role & Examples & Authority\\\midrule
Deterministic control & Identity, validation, receipts, replay rules & Declared rules only\\
Conventional fitted ML & Ridge, tree models, MLPs & Scientific output under frozen protocol\\
Optional generative assistance & Orientation, critique, diagnosis, proposals & Advisory; detachable\\
Human authority & Protocol freeze, interpretation, engineering approval & Final bounded decision\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Worked public example: a bounded furnace workflow}
The furnace viewport study shows how a completed calculation can remain ineligible for fabrication. This is public contract-level semantics, not private orchestration.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Declare the case.} Corrected interface geometry: 35--40 mm. Source assumptions and thermal boundaries are declared. Requested decision: is the concept mature enough to fabricate or test?
\item \textbf{Bind the computation.} A deterministic thermal model, candidate families, uncertainty/failure scenarios, and expected outputs are declared.
\item \textbf{Run and retain failure.} Baseline response, contact/seal/emissivity uncertainty, active-cooling-loss, and edge/frame-transfer uncertainty remain visible.
\item \textbf{Compare predecessor evidence.} The inherited 59.2~$^\circ$C result belonged to a 350--375 mm geometry. The corrected geometry creates a different scientific identity, so the prior result is retained historically but cannot transfer silently.
\item \textbf{Adjudicate.} The model may be numerically complete while interface and cooling dependencies remain unresolved. Outcome: HOLD.
\item \textbf{Authorise only the bounded action.} A guarded opaque thermal-interface coupon is authorised; full fabrication, furnace deployment, and qualification are not.
\end{enumerate}

uncertainty_state: unresolved_edge_and_cooling_dependency

\subsection{How to translate this to another engineering workflow}
Ask five questions: What exact object is being evaluated? What source or configuration makes this run scientifically distinct? Which failures must remain visible rather than averaged away? Which predecessor evidence may transfer, and what changed enough to break transfer? What is the smallest next action the evidence justifies?

\section{Conclusion}
The Evidence-State Runtime is an assurance architecture around scientific computation, not another scheduler and not yet a universal production platform. It makes identity, completion, recovery, replay, memory, failure, and action permission separately inspectable.

Its practical industrial proposition is incremental. Existing scientific and AI-assisted workflows can be wrapped so that interruption, repeatability, retained evidence, and human permission cease to be implicit. The system does not make every computation trustworthy. It makes the state of a computation clear enough that trust and escalation need not be inferred from successful termination alone.

\callout{Bottom line.}{Preserve the work. Preserve the failure. Preserve the distinction between evidence and permission.}

\textbf{If you remember one thing:} the runtime should make the earned evidence state visible without allowing execution, replay, AI assistance, or narrative confidence to authorise a stronger action automatically.
